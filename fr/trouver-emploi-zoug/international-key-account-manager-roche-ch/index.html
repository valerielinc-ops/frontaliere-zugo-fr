The Position A healthier future. It’s what drives us to innovate. To continuously advance science and ensure everyone has access to the healthcare they need today and for generations to come. Creating a world where we all have more time with the people we love. That’s what makes us Roche.

The Opportunity As an International Key Account Manager at Roche Diagnostics you will be responsible for setting the strategic direction and the impact of the strategic partnership with an International Key Account, based in Europe with international presence and strategic focus in LATAM. You act as an agile leader and engage internally & externally through the actions below to enable a successful partnership with the International Key Account (IKA) and deliver impact:

  • Own the enterprise level account responsibility and its strategy by being accountable for the strategic partnership, customer satisfaction, profit and loss, ensure the IKA consistently achieves or exceeds annual revenue, market share, and other relevant goals, cohesive across countries and territories. Create opportunities, and continously grow our commercial baseline and strategic partnership with the IKA
  • Represent Roche and establish trustful relationships with all C-Levels of the international account and senior stakeholders at the customer level
  • Collaborate with all relevant internal teams to achieve an outstanding customer experience, commercial impact and business development, working closely with country representatives (key account managers, commercial leaders) and global/regional teams
  • Accountable for integrating and aligning cross-functional and integrated diagnostics strategies and identify strategic opportunities to enable our affiliates to a winning collaboration with the IKA
  • Consistently leverage data and insights on marketing trends, competitive dynamics, and pricing to develop strategy and decision support
  • Create visibility and excitement, engaging the organization around the account and share best practices, learnings, progress across the network
  • Contribute to the broader network strategy to develop international key accounts beyond the assigned IKA, identifying emerging opportunities across EMEA-LATAM

Who you are

To be successful in this role, you ideally bring the following skills and experiences:

  • Experience working in an international, multi-cultural, and matrix environment
  • Strategic thinking skills and proven track record of developing and delivering business strategies on enterprise level
  • You have a knack for business development and don’t shy away from trying new approaches, leveraging your agile leadership skills (VACC) to find creative solutions
  • You are creative leader of networked teams, leading and coaching affiliates and stakeholders around the international key account with a unified strategy and vision
  • Possess business acumen and commercial experience, including multi-country tenders and large scale deals
  • You are a people person, emphatetic and able to build strong, trusted relationship. You support your peers, you bring a positive energy and a forward looking approach to the teams you are a part of. You understand the customer, and the challenges they face, working with, not only for, the customer
  • Demonstrate a passion for leading change, enjoys negotiating, and has a natural ability to manage conflict
  • You have an intricate motivation to deploy truly integrated solutions and strategic partnership s within the eco-system of healthcare, which benefit patients across the world

This roles requires willingness to travel around 25%,. Please note that at times you might travel more while building relationships with the customers and countries.

Where pay transparency applies, details are provided based on the primary posting location. For this role, the primary location is Rotkreuz.
