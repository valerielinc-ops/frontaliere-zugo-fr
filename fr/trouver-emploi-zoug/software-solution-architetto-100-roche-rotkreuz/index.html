The Position

In a global, cross-functional environment, the PCR Software Development chapter develops, delivers and and maintains integrated, unified and reliable software solutions for Molecular Diagnostics Systems by leveraging our advanced capabilities, in-depth expertise and strong internal & external partnerships driving the accuracy, efficiency and agility in innovating the Molecular Lab experience for our customers and patients throughout their journey.

As a Software Solution Architect, you will report to the PCR SW Chapter Lead and play a pivotal role in enabling effective collaboration across teams. You will lead the architecture, design, development, testing, maintenance and enhancement of large-scale software solutions spanning multiple teams and value streams. Your contributions will directly support the Diagnostics 10-year ambition by enabling our Molecular Lab network to deliver accurate and timely diagnostic solutions.

Operating at the SAFe Solution Train level, you will contribute to the architectural vision and guide multiple Agile Release Trains (ARTs) building the MolLab digital portfolio. You will lead the end-to-end architectural strategy for integrating MolLab solutions into the global Roche navify digital ecosystem.

The Opportunity

In this position, you will be challenged in the following areas:

  • Developing a broad understanding of the Roche ecosystem and product landscape, specifically within the MolLab PCR context ensuring the solutions fit the broader enterprise and product ecosystem.
  • Supporting business teams in solution definition and system integration to translate long-term business strategy into actionable capabilities.
  • Contributing to the Architecture & Technology (A&T) discipline through cross-project alignment and robust API definition.
  • Establishes and governs comprehensive design documentation and modeling practices to ensure architectural clarity, continuity and seamless technical alignment across multiple teams and projects.
  • Mentoring and coaching colleagues, nurturing their potential and guiding them in architectural decision-making.
  • Exploring, defining, and leveraging Agentic AI capabilities to drive continuous innovation.
  • Proactively staying up to date with emerging technologies, industry trends and evolving software architecture practices to drive continuous innovation.
  • Leading the definition of the Solution Intent and establishing the Solution-level Architectural Runway, balancing long-term intentional architecture with emergent design across delivery teams.
  • Collaborating closely with Solution Management to define, prioritize, and champion enablers in the Solution Train Backlog that support future business features to address solution-level technical debt.

Who you are

You as a candidate for our dynamic team will have the following skills and experience:

  • Demonstrated extensive expertise in definition and building of architectures for large-scale, modern software solutions spanning multiple Agile Release Trains (ARTs) and value streams.
  • Strong expertise in SW engineering and other functions such as system- and requirements engineering and V&V. Demonstrated experience with software development in multiple programming languages.
  • Proven experience navigating and shaping strategic initiatives and software architecture within the evolving regulatory landscape of medical software development.
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to negotiate complex concepts and architectural decisions, foster an inclusive environment and establish a psychologically safe learning culture.
  • A leadership style that empowers teams, prioritizes cross-functional value delivery and creates the conditions for ownership and accountability.
  • A Master’s degree or equivalent practical experience in the specialized field of Software Development / Engineering and experience in IVD software development is a plus for succeeding in the role.
  • Relevant industry certifications, including iSAQB Advanced Level, SEI Software Architecture Professional or TOGAF.
