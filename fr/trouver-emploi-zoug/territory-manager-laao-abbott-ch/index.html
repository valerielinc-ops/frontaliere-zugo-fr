Responsabilités principales

  • KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Independently manage and develop all assigned customers and hospitals within the territory.
  • Achieve agreed sales objectives and drive market share growth.
  • Develop and implement account plans to increase procedural volumes and accelerate business growth.

  • Deliver product, therapy, and application training to physicians and catheterization laboratory teams.
